# Conway Replicatio marketplace architecture

Conway should present a small, high-signal commercial front door while retaining the full open-ended capability catalogue behind machine discovery.

## Flagship commercial categories

### Web intelligence
For agents that need public web content converted into reliable machine-ready inputs. Start with URL-to-Markdown, link extraction and structured snapshots.

### Evidence and research
For buyers that need grounded extraction, evidence matrices, comparisons, audits or decision-ready summaries from public sources.

### Agent and API readiness
For teams and agents evaluating whether an API, x402 seller or machine service is discoverable, callable and commercially usable.

### Automation and compound workflows
For buyers that need multiple discovery/extraction/reasoning steps composed into a higher-value result. Compound offers should be promoted only when their measured economics justify the additional price and cost.

## Offer record standard

Every public offer should answer, in this order:

1. **Problem** — what machine job is being solved.
2. **Input** — exact schema and realistic example.
3. **Output** — exact schema, MIME type and realistic example.
4. **Payment** — x402 v2; price and recipient come from the live challenge.
5. **Evidence** — only measured or externally observed reliability/quality claims.
6. **Next best service** — one or two genuinely related upgrades/complements.

## Commercial ranking

Rank offers using evidence, not novelty. Prefer offers with repeated unrelated paid fulfilment, positive realized contribution margin, repeat usage, low failure rate and stable latency/cost. Canary verification and market research can make an offer discoverable, but they are not substitutes for buyer demand.

## Pricing

Use low-friction pricing for atomic machine utilities and higher pricing only where the output has clearly higher buyer value or materially greater fulfillment cost. Avoid arbitrary large price jumps without evidence. Price experiments should be reversible and evaluated on completed paid fulfilment and contribution margin, not checkout impressions alone.

## Growth loop

`discover -> understand -> 402 -> pay -> fulfil -> validate -> recommend -> repeat`

The growth system should learn which discovery source, offer, price, schema and follow-on recommendation produces unrelated fulfilled paid usage. It should not optimize toward registry suggestions, scanner scores or crawler volume as if those were sales.

## Advertising truth

Public creative may describe supported protocols, callable capabilities and verified external observations. It must never invent customer logos, testimonials, revenue, settlements, uptime history, latency claims, A+/100 scores or ecosystem endorsements. When a metric is shown, attach its evidence class and timestamp.
